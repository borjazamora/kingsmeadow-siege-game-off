    How to play
    • Drive with the left stick. Fire with your right thumb: drag anywhere on the right half of the screen to aim, let go to fire.
    • Story: pick your path across the island to the Big Gun. Waves: endless waves, with a Big Gun every fifth. Siege: guard the keep in the middle, or storm castles with two allies. It never ends: every boss you beat starts a tougher stage, until the keep falls or you do.
    • Story is a road of 24 levels across six islands, each island ending in a boss. Harbour Isle has powder kegs that blow up when shot, Dune Isle has quicksand that pulls you in, Jungle Isle has rivers you cross on bridges that fit one cannon at a time, Highland Isle has wind that bends your shots, and Frost Isle has slippery ice. The Daily borrows a different island each day. Clear a level to open the next one. Your upgrades carry from level to level on the same island, and each new island starts fresh. Clear a level and you keep the cannon you finished it with. Earn up to three stars a level: clear it, lose 3 hearts or fewer, lose 1 or none. Stars unlock skins. Every cannon is yours from the start.
    • The Flamethrower only works up close: it sprays fire in a short cone in front of you and sets cannons alight. Fire goes through bushes but not tall rocks.
    • After each fight you pick one of three upgrades. They stack, so mix and match.
    • Tall rocks stop cannonballs, low ones don't. Bushes hide you until you fire, and hide enemies too. Tap a bush to shoot into it. Mud slows you down.
    • Zoom with the + and − buttons or the mouse wheel. On a computer, drag with the right mouse button (or hold Q or E) to turn the camera.
    • Lobbers fire over rocks: get off the red ring before the shell lands. Longshots show a red line before they fire, and hit for two hearts: get out of the line.
    • Brutes, Lobbers and Longshots sometimes drop their barrel. Drive over it to switch your cannon to that type for the rest of the run. Your upgrades come with you.
    • Siege has two sides. Defend the keep, or Storm the castle: you and two allies attack a castle that gets bigger every round. Break the stones one by one, smash the gate to stop cannons coming out, watch out for the towers, and knock down the keep inside to win the round.
    • Tap the right side to fire at the closest cannon you can see. Hidden, off-screen or behind-a-rock cannons are skipped, so aim those yourself.
    • The radar on the left shows the whole island: you in gold, enemies in red, allies in blue. Cannons hiding in bushes don't show.
    • Every third wave night falls (except in Story). In the dark you only see cannons close to you, or ones that just fired, and they can't see you either. Fireflies come out too. Use + and − to zoom.
    • Daily: six waves on today's island, the same for everyone, with a boss at the end. You pick an upgrade after each wave. Everyone uses the Classic cannon on difficulty 1.
    • Every knocked-out cannon drops a heart. Clearing a wave gives you two back.
    • Score comes from knockouts, clearing waves, clearing them fast, and clearing them without getting hit. Higher difficulty scores more.

    Keyboard: WASD or arrows to drive. Swipe or click with the mouse to fire.
